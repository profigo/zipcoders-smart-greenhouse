How the system works

01 · SENSE

ESP32 reads temperature, humidity and soil moisture sensors.

02 · DECIDE

Rules compare readings with farmer-defined thresholds.

03 · ACT

Relays can switch irrigation and ventilation equipment.

04 · INFORM
